Weld Joint

By Kabelfrikkler. Based on Box Joint by Mark Suska (MIT License).

Creates weld tabs and finger joints between plates that are cut by laser or waterjet and then welded. The joint is a single, editable feature in the timeline.

Usage

  1. Model the plates with simple butt joints: plates touch each other with a planar face.
  2. Open Weld Joint (DESIGN workspace, SOLID tab, MODIFY panel).
  3. Select the outside face of each plate to join.
  4. Choose the Mode and set the values, then click OK.

A joint is created wherever a selected plate butts against another selected plate, at any angle. The butting plate gets the tabs, the other plate gets the slots. Material thickness is measured from the plates.

To change a joint, select it in the timeline and choose Edit Feature. The dialog remembers the last used settings, also across documents.

Modes

FingersAlternating fingers along the whole edge.
Tabs: SpacingTabs of a fixed width, spread evenly. All gaps, also those to the ends of the edge, are equal and never larger than Tab Spacing.
Tabs: PointsOne tab at each selected sketch point, centered along the edge. Draw the points in a sketch on an outside face, on the edge or within the plate thickness. Dimension them in the sketch: the tabs follow when the sketch changes.

Inputs

FacesOutside faces of the plates to join.
ModeSee above.
Tab PointsSketch or construction points that place the tabs (mode Tabs: Points).
Min / Max FingersNumber of fingers of both plates together (odd).
Min / Max Finger WidthAllowed finger width.
Finger RatioWidth share of the fingers on the butting plate. 0.5 makes all fingers equally wide.
Tab WidthWidth of each tab along the edge.
Tab SpacingLargest gap between two tabs.
Corner MarginMoves all fingers or tabs inward by this distance, from both ends of the edge.
Tab SetbackThe tabs end this far below the outside face of the other plate, which leaves room for the weld.
Slot ClearancePlay along the edge, per side: each slot is longer than its tab by this value on both sides.
Hole ClearancePlay across the edge, per side: each slot is wider than the plate thickness by this value on both sides. Only for closed holes inside a face, not for notches at a corner.

Installation / Uninstallation

Install: copy the folder WeldJoint.bundle to %appdata%\Autodesk\ApplicationPlugins (Windows) or ~/Library/Application Support/Autodesk/ApplicationPlugins (macOS) and start Fusion.

Uninstall: close Fusion and delete the folder. To stop the Add-In without uninstalling it, use UTILITIES > Scripts and Add-Ins.
